What is stored and what is not.
Last updated October 2026

What is stored

Only when you submit to a ranking, the name and score you enter are stored on our servers. Some works store a little more, as described below.

Sushi Stack also stores a number generated on your device and the move history of that run. The history is used to check that the score adds up. The device number is a random string created on your device and is used only to group records from the same device. It does not identify anyone.

Stop at 10 seconds stores only your name, the errors of your three tries and the time you submitted. It does not create a device number. The day’s ranking is cleared when the date changes.

Negaeri Shogi, when you play online (and when you open the ranking), stores a device-generated number, your name (default: “Anonymous” in Japanese), your rating, win/loss counts, and the moves and result of each game. Your name and rating appear in the ranking. Playing against the AI, or two players on the same device, stores nothing.

Anime Type Quiz calculates your answers on your device only and sends them nowhere.

You can choose any name. You do not need to use your real name.

What is not stored

Nothing else is collected. There is no registration and no mechanism to track individuals. We do not receive your email address, phone number or location.

The site runs Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts how many times pages are opened and which pages are read. It is not used to track individuals or for advertising.

What stays only on your device

High scores, personal bests, collection data, language and sound settings stay only on your device. If you do not submit to a ranking, nothing leaves your device. Clearing your browser’s storage removes it.

Comments on articles

Some articles have a comments section. Only when you press “Open comments” does your browser connect to giscus (giscus.app) and GitHub. If you do not press it, no connection is made. Writing a comment requires a GitHub account, and comments are public, stored in GitHub Discussions with your GitHub username. You can delete your own comment on GitHub.

If you want something deleted

To remove a record you submitted to a ranking, contact the address on the About page (Japanese) with the name you used. We will confirm and delete it.

Where it is hosted

This site and the ranking servers run on Cloudflare. Sushi Stack and Negaeri Shogi rankings are stored in Cloudflare D1 (a database), and Stop at 10 seconds in Cloudflare KV.

About advertising

When our games are published on other sites (game portals), that site’s own advertising systems may apply, under that site’s policy.

This site shows ads through Google AdSense. Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s previous visits to this site or other sites. Details are in Google’s advertising policies.

You can turn off personalized advertising at any time in Google Ad Settings, or opt out of third-party vendors’ cookies at aboutads.info. Visitors in the European Economic Area and the UK first see a consent prompt provided by Google. We do not receive cookie information for any purpose other than ads.

Children

We do not ask for age and do not collect information related to age. Apart from the name you put in a ranking, nothing reaches us.
