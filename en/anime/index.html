How the quiz works

The Anime Type Quiz measures your taste in anime on six axes, from 12 up to 50 multiple-choice questions. Each axis gets one letter for the side you lean to, and the letters together form your type code (for example RDCP-VM). Every axis has two sides, so there are 26 = 64 types.

Every anime in the quiz’s database has values on the same six axes. Your result is chosen by treating your six values and each anime’s six values as vectors and measuring how close they are (cosine similarity).

The six axes

WorldR / F

Is the setting an extension of reality, or another world? Realistic or Fantasy.

AftertasteD / H

After it ends, do you want weight or a lift? Dark or Happy.

Structure & paceC / S

Do you want to pick up foreshadowing, or ride the momentum? Foreshadowing or Pace & momentum.

Sub-tasteP / A

Is the highlight a battle of wits, or action? Mind games or Action.

Visual vs storyV / T

What decides it for you: the visuals or the script? Visuals or Story.

Viewing styleM / L

Do you savor it slowly, or enjoy it casually? Deep-dive or Binge.
