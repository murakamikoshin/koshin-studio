Koshin Studio

A one-person studio. I build browser games and small apps, and write down what I measure.

Free. No install, no sign-up.

Play

  • Sushi Stack — A falling-block puzzle: collide matching sushi toppings to grow them, all the way up to the golden sushi tub. Available in 14 languages, opens in your browser language. (About, English)
  • Negaeri Shogi — A 6×6 board game. Pieces you sandwich defect to your side and get promoted, and chains flip many at once. Local, vs AI, and online with ratings. The game is in English and Japanese. (rules, English)
  • Stop at 10 seconds — Count ten seconds in your head and stop. Best of three; the smaller the error, the stronger you are. No setup, no sign-up. The interface is in Japanese. (About, English)
  • Anime Type Quiz — 12 questions, 6 axes: your anime type and the one anime that fits you right now. Free, nothing is sent anywhere. In English.
  • トレカダッシュ (Toreca Dash) — Lottery, pre-order and restock alerts for trading cards sold in Japan.

Tools

  • Gacha completion calculator — How many pulls, and how much money, to collect every type?
  • Gacha drop rate and pity calculator — From the drop rate, how many pulls until you get it?
  • Lottery win chance calculator — From the odds and the number of entries, the chance you win at least once.
