What it is

Sales of trading cards in Japan are announced one store at a time, on store sites and on X. By the time you notice, the deadline has passed. Toreca Dash collects announcements from stores and online shops across Japan and takes you straight to the page where you can apply or buy.

Main features

Feed

See open lotteries, pre-orders and first-come sales, filtered by game and sale type.

Alerts

Get notified as soon as something new appears for a product or game you follow.

Deadline calendar

Lottery deadlines and result dates, day by day, so you do not miss an entry.

Entry tracker

Record the lotteries you entered, and the results and purchases.

Map

Share in-store stock and lottery information with other users.

Supported games

Pokémon Card Game, Yu-Gi-Oh!, ONE PIECE Card Game, Union Arena, Lorcana, Duel Masters, Dragon Ball, Gundam, Magic: The Gathering, Weiss Schwarz, Detective Conan, hololive, Digimon, Haikyu!! Babo-ca!!, Palworld (15 in total).

Toreca Dash Pro (optional, paid)

  • Unlimited product follows, keyword alerts and excluded keywords.
  • The fastest alerts for first-come and surprise sales (the free version is about 15 minutes behind).
  • Filter to stores near your location.
  • Full notification history, entry statistics, calendar export and CSV export.
  • Track the value and profit/loss of your collection from purchase records.
  • No ads.

Good to know before you use it

  • Store information is mostly in Japanese. Store names, announcements and product names are shown as published in Japan.
  • Many Japanese stores require a Japanese address, phone number or store membership to enter a lottery. Check the conditions on each store’s own page before you apply.
  • The app shows the information and sends you to the store. It does not buy or enter lotteries for you.
  • Screens are available in Japanese and English. More languages are being added.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. The main features are free. Pro is an optional paid plan.

Do I need an account?

No. The feed, alerts and calendar work without signing in. Sign in with Apple is only used for map posts and the daily fortune.

Is it an official app?

No. It is an unofficial app made by an individual, and is not affiliated with any card game publisher or store. Information comes from stores’ public announcements; always confirm the latest details on the store’s official page before you apply or buy.
