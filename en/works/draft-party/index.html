What it is

Name your team and pick fictional rookies. In Round 1 everyone bids at the same time, hidden from the others, and all bids are revealed together. If teams chose the same player, they draw lots: the competing teams take turns picking one hidden paper, and when everyone has picked, all papers open at once. Only the winner gets the rights; the others re-bid on a different player.

From Round 2, teams pick one at a time, and the order flips every round. At the end, the contract talks are announced — “signed” or “refused”. You can turn your result into an image and share it on X.

Two modes

Scout mode

First, each participant raises their own player through three years of high school. Form and luck change the results, and training the same stat repeatedly can unlock special skills. The raised players join everyone else’s and the CPU’s in the draft, and when one is picked, its owner decides whether to sign or refuse.

Pick fictional rookies by their abilities, potential and records. The abilities are the scouts’ estimates and can differ from reality. Signed players grow over several years while every team plays every other team. You get yearly standings, head-to-head records against every team, titles such as batting champion and wins leader, Rookie of the Year and draft grades — revealed to everyone at the same time, so you can react together with stamps and chat. The host can also register the players.

Choice mode

Pick from a list the host prepared — for example, everyone in your class or 50 favorite anime. Great for groups of friends.

With friends, or with strangers

  • Friends room: enter with a 6-character room code or a link. The host freely chooses the number of teams, rounds, time limit, refusals and mode, and nothing starts until the host presses Start. A public room is listed so strangers can join. The host can also remove anyone who causes trouble.
  • Find an open room: browse public rooms and see their rules (teams, rounds, time limit, refusals) before you join.
  • Quick join: don’t want to search? One tap puts you in an open room, or starts one with CPU teams so there is no wait.

Every team gets an original emblem automatically (you can pick another), and you can use your own image in any room (inappropriate images are hidden after reports).

How it is built

The server (Cloudflare Workers with Durable Objects) runs all the progress and judging. Round 1 bids are not sent to anyone until the reveal, and the winning paper of a lottery is known only to the server until everyone has drawn. Even the host cannot peek at bids or rig a lottery. There is no login, and the sound effects and music are generated in the browser (no audio files).

The players in Scout mode are fictional people generated at random each time. They have no connection to real players, teams or results, and this game has no connection to any professional league or team.

What is stored

For the sake of play inside a room, a device-generated number, your team name, what you pick, chat messages and the room settings are kept only for the life of that room, and are all deleted 10 minutes after the game ends. There is no ranking. Details are on the privacy page.
