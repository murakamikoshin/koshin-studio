Draft Party

Pick any word you like. Tie with someone? Lottery!
A draft day for everyone, in your browser.

What it is

Name your team and pick words. In Round 1 everyone bids at the same time, hidden from the others, and all bids are revealed together. If teams chose the same word, they draw lots: the competing teams take turns picking one hidden paper, and when everyone has picked, all papers open at once. Only the winner gets the rights; the others re-bid with a different word.

From Round 2, teams pick one at a time, and the order flips every round. At the end, the contract talks are announced — “signed” or “refused”. You can turn your result into an image and share it on X.

Two modes

Free-word mode

Pick anything — a person, a food, any word, not just baseball. The host can set a theme, or everyone can vote for one.

Scout mode

Pick fictional rookies by their abilities, potential and records. The abilities are the scouts’ estimates and can differ from reality. Signed players grow over several years while every team plays every other team. You get yearly standings, head-to-head records against every team, titles such as batting champion and wins leader, Rookie of the Year and draft grades — revealed to everyone at the same time, so you can react together with stamps and chat. There is also a mode where the host registers the players.

With friends, or with strangers

  • Friends room: enter with a 6-character room code or a link. The host chooses the number of teams, rounds, time limit, mode and theme, and nothing starts until the host presses Start. Once your friends are in, the host can make the room public so random-match players can join. The host can also remove anyone who causes trouble.
  • Random match: you are grouped with players using the same language. If there aren’t enough players, CPU teams join, so it starts without a wait.

Every team gets an original emblem automatically (you can pick another), and you can use your own image in any room (inappropriate images are hidden after reports).

How it is built

The server (Cloudflare Workers with Durable Objects) runs all the progress and judging. Round 1 bids are not sent to anyone until the reveal, and the winning paper of a lottery is known only to the server until everyone has drawn. Even the host cannot peek at bids or rig a lottery. There is no login, and the sound effects and music are generated in the browser (no audio files).

The players in Scout mode are fictional people generated at random each time. They have no connection to real players, teams or results, and this game has no connection to any professional league or team.

What is stored

For the sake of play inside a room, a device-generated number, your team name, what you pick, chat messages and the room settings are kept only for the life of that room, and are all deleted 10 minutes after the game ends. There is no ranking. Details are on the privacy page.
