What it is

Tube Drift is a 3D endless runner where you fly down a neon tunnel. The only control is a left-right swipe: spin the tunnel around your finger and slide the ship through the gaps in the walls rushing toward you.

Hit a wall and the run is over. A run lasts about 30 seconds, so it fits a commute or one last round before bed.

How to score

The closer to the wall, the more you earn

Slipping right past a wall gives a near-miss bonus. Chain them and your combo climbs, raising the multiplier up to ×8.

FEVER doubles it again

Reach a combo of 10 and FEVER starts: about 7 seconds of double score. One mistake breaks the combo, so deciding when to push is the skill.

Collect gems, change the scenery

Gems float in the tunnel. Your running total unlocks new themes: Sunset, Deep Sea, Jade and White Night. Same controls, different mood.

Daily missions

Three missions rotate every day, such as “score 1500 in one run”, “8 near-misses” or “reach combo 12”. Complete them for bonus gems.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. No install, no sign-up. High scores and gems are saved only on your device.

Which devices work?

Browsers on iPhone and Android. It also runs on a computer, but swiping on a phone in portrait is the most comfortable.

Is there sound?

Yes, music and effects, with a toggle at the bottom right. There are no audio files; everything is synthesized on the spot.

How it is made

Rendering uses three.js, and the whole game fits in a single HTML file. The music is synthesized live and gets thicker with your combo and FEVER.
